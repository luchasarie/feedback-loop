# Community standard

People arrive here because an agent failed them. Frustration is allowed. Harassment of contributors is not.

Be direct about behavior, evidence, impact, and desired outcomes. Do not attack people for identity, background, or ability. Do not publish private transcripts, credentials, or identifying data to prove a point. Maintainers may remove content that crosses those boundaries.

The product does not tone-police users. The project community still protects people.
